What is it about

Vanilla.
But different and better.

Runewake is an upcoming Minecraft RPG project inspired by it's first experimental version Legends of the North RPG plugin and Skyrim with more functions and different stories to try.

Main story & quests

Keep your gameplay alive with side quests while you're doing the Main story. You won't be bored because there will be always something to do.

Shouts and powers

Powerful shouts and power which you can use to defeat bosses or even players. Destroy, heal, or make animals fight along with you.

Be different

Learn different shouts, do different quests or chose different way to play.
